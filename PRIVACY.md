# Global User Data Protection & Privacy Policy — OMARVIO BROWSER

**Effective Worldwide · Applies to Android APK, Windows PC, Linux Desktop, and Web PWA**

## 1. Zero Telemetry, Zero Surveillance & No Data Selling
- OMARVIO GLOBAL LTD. **does not** collect, log, profile, rent, or sell your browsing history, search queries, IP addresses, bookmarks, downloads, or device identifiers.
- No Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Facebook SDK, crash-reporting beacons, or third-party ad trackers are bundled in our Web PWA, Windows/Linux Desktop client, or Android APK.

## 2. 100% On-Device Storage & Encrypted Vault
- All open tabs, browsing history, saved bookmarks, custom home shortcuts, reader archives, and site permissions live strictly inside local storage on your phone or computer.
- Your **Privacy Vault** (PIN / Biometric protected on Android) never transmits credentials or secrets to external servers.
- **Incognito Tabs** retain zero persistent navigation history after the tab or session is closed.

## 3. Search, Direct Web Browsing & AI Privacy
- **Direct Native Loading:** Android APK and Windows/Linux Desktop load external websites directly through Chromium (`WebView` / `WebContentsView`) while blocking ad/tracking domains locally before requests leave your device.
- **Local Parallel Search:** Desktop IPC and client-side CORS calls query public APIs directly from your device without touching an OMARVIO tracking server. Any edge search fallback uses short-lived anonymous HTTP response caching with zero user/IP logs.
- **On-Device & External AI:** Local **Ollama (`127.0.0.1`)** and **Gemini Nano (`window.ai`)** run strictly on your hardware. External AI services (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Duck.ai, etc.) open directly on their official domains under your control.
- **Tracker Stripping & HTTPS-Only:** Tracking query parameters (`utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`) are stripped automatically and HTTP connections upgrade to HTTPS whenever supported.

## 4. Worldwide User Rights (GDPR, CCPA & International Standards)
Regardless of your country or region, you maintain full sovereignty over your data:
- **Access & Inspection:** Review bookmarks, downloads, history, and HTTPS exceptions anytime in Settings.
- **Instant Erasure:** Use **Clear Browsing Data** or the **Panic Wipe** control to immediately erase tabs, history, and local site caches.
- **Global Privacy Signals:** `DNT: 1` (Do Not Track) and `Sec-GPC: 1` (Global Privacy Control) signals are respected across supported engines.
